Available Incentives

IN REBATES

San Francisco and Marin County homeowners can capture significant federal, state, and local incentives for green building upgrades. SolidBay helps you identify and claim every dollar available.

🇺🇸

Federal Tax Credits (IRA)

The Inflation Reduction Act provides up to $3,200/year in federal tax credits for energy-efficient home improvements including heat pumps, insulation, windows, and electrical panel upgrades.

Up to $3,200/year
🌴

TECH Clean California

State rebates for heat pump water heaters ($1,000–$3,500) and heat pump HVAC systems ($1,000–$4,500). Available to all California homeowners regardless of income.

Up to $8,000 in rebates
⚡

PG&E Energy Efficiency

PG&E offers rebates for insulation upgrades, smart thermostats, EV charger installation, and energy-efficient appliances for SF and Marin County customers.

Various rebates available
🌿

Marin Clean Energy

Marin County's clean energy provider offers additional incentives for all-electric homes, EV charging, and battery storage — on top of state and federal programs.

Additional Marin incentives

Our Commitment

PURSUING GREEN
CERTIFICATION

SolidBay Construction SF is actively pursuing CGBP (Certified Green Building Professional) certification — the leading green building credential for Bay Area contractors. We are also working toward GreenPoint Rated certification, California's primary residential green building rating system.

In the meantime, our team already applies sustainable building practices on every project — from specifying low-VOC materials and high-efficiency fixtures to designing for solar readiness and all-electric operation. Green building is not a certification for us — it's a commitment built into everything we do.
