COMMON QUESTIONS

How much does a home renovation cost in Richmond District?

Renovation costs in Richmond District typically run $300–$500/sq ft for mid-range work and $500–$800/sq ft for luxury finishes. Kitchen remodels start around $70,000+ and ADUs typically run $180,000–$350,000.

Do I need a permit for renovation in Richmond District?

Yes — any structural, electrical, plumbing, or mechanical work in San Francisco requires permits from SF's DBI. Our in-house permit expeditor handles all applications and inspections for every SolidBay project in Richmond District.

How long does a renovation take in Richmond District?

Kitchen remodels typically take 3–5 months, bathroom renovations 6–10 weeks, and full home renovations 9–18 months including permitting. We provide realistic timelines upfront — no surprises.

Is SolidBay licensed and insured in Richmond District?

Yes. SolidBay Construction SF holds a valid California CSLB license and carries full General Liability and Workers' Compensation insurance. We serve all San Francisco neighborhoods including Richmond District.
