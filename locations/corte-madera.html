How much does a home renovation cost in Corte Madera?

Renovation costs in Corte Madera are comparable to the broader Bay Area market — typically $400–$700/sq ft for mid-range work and $700–$1,200+/sq ft for luxury finishes. Kitchen remodels start around $75,000 and ADUs typically run $200,000–$450,000.

Do I need a permit for renovation in Corte Madera?

Yes — most structural, electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work requires permits. Our in-house permit expeditor handles all permit applications and inspections for every Solid Bay project in Corte Madera.

How long does a renovation take in Corte Madera?

Timeline depends on scope: kitchen remodels typically take 3–5 months, bathroom renovations 6–10 weeks, and full home renovations 9–18 months including permitting. We provide realistic timelines upfront — no surprises.

Is Solid Bay licensed and insured in Corte Madera?

Yes. Solid Bay Construction SF holds a valid California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) license and carries full General Liability ($2M+) and Workers' Compensation insurance. We serve all of Marin County including Corte Madera.
