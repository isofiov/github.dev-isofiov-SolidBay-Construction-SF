Who We Are

Two Partners.
One Mission.
100+ Projects.

SolidBay Construction SF was founded in 2016 by two partners who shared a simple belief: that renovation in San Francisco deserved a firm combining design intelligence, engineering precision, and permitting expertise — all under one roof.

Over the past decade, we've delivered more than 100 residential and commercial renovation projects across San Francisco and Marin County.

  • 2 Founding Partners with 10+ years in SF renovation
  • 100+ completed projects — SF & Marin County
  • Residential & commercial renovation specialists
  • Full in-house team: design, engineering & permitting
  • California General Contractor Licensed & Bonded
