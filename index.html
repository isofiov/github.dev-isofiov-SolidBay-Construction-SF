Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

Everything San Francisco and Marin County homeowners and businesses need to know before starting a renovation project.

How much does a home renovation cost in San Francisco?

Costs range from $150–$250/sq ft for cosmetic work, $300–$500/sq ft for mid-level renovation, and $500–$800/sq ft for high-end projects. A kitchen remodel typically runs $85,000–$250,000 and a full home renovation can reach $700K–$1.5M.

How long do San Francisco building permits take?

Simple over-the-counter permits take 1–5 business days. DBI plan check takes 4–10 weeks. Projects requiring Planning review take 8–16 weeks. Our dedicated in-house expeditor cuts these timelines by 30–50%.

Do I need a permit for renovation in San Francisco?

Yes — almost any structural change, electrical upgrade, plumbing modification, or addition requires a permit from SF's Department of Building Inspection (DBI). Some projects also need Planning Department approval. Our expeditor handles all permitting for SolidBay clients.

Can I build an ADU in San Francisco?

Yes! Under current California and SF law, most single-family properties can build one ADU and one Junior ADU by right — no discretionary hearing needed. A garage conversion ADU typically costs $180,000–$350,000 and can add $200,000–$500,000+ in property value.

Does SolidBay Construction serve Marin County?

Absolutely. We serve both San Francisco and Marin County — including Mill Valley, Sausalito, Tiburon, Corte Madera, San Anselmo, Fairfax, and Larkspur. We're fully versed in Marin's WUI fire safety requirements and each municipality's permitting process.

How do I choose the right SF renovation contractor?

Verify the California contractor's license at cslb.ca.gov, confirm general liability ($1M+) and workers' comp insurance, ask for SF project references, and ensure they understand SF's complex permitting process. A boutique firm with in-house architects, engineers, and an expeditor is a major advantage.
