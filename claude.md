# SolidBay Construction SF — Claude Rules

## Brand Identity
- Company: SolidBay Construction SF
- Founded: 2016
- Location: 2181 Greenwich St, San Francisco, CA 94123
- Service Areas: San Francisco & Marin County
- Specialty: Residential & Commercial Renovation
- Team: 2 Partners, 2 Designers, 2 Structural Engineers, 2 Architects, 1 Expeditor
- Projects: 100+ completed

## Brand Colors
- Forest Green (primary): #2E6B3E
- Deep Green: #1e4f2c
- Gold (primary accent): #C9A84C
- Dark Gold: #A8872E
- Bright Gold: #D4A843
- Cream (background): #F2EFE6
- Off-White: #FAF8F3
- Dark Text: #1a2e1a
- Gray Text: #6b7c6b
- Border: rgba(46,107,62,0.15)

## Typography
- Display font: 'Bebas Neue' (headings, hero)
- Body font: 'DM Sans' (body text, UI)
- Serif accent: Georgia (logo wordmark only)

## Design Principles
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Clean, professional, luxury construction aesthetic
- Cream/warm background (NOT dark background)
- Green and gold accents throughout
- Consistent 8px spacing system
- Smooth CSS animations and transitions
- No purple gradients, no generic AI aesthetics

## Framework
- Tailwind CSS via CDN
- Vanilla JavaScript (no frameworks)
- SVG for logo and icons
- Unsplash for placeholder images

## Layout Rules
- Max content width: 1280px
- Mobile breakpoint: 768px
- Tablet breakpoint: 1024px
- Section padding: py-20 (mobile: py-12)
- Container padding: px-6 (desktop: px-12)

## Components
- Navbar: Fixed, cream background, logo left, links right
- Buttons: Solid green primary, gold ghost secondary
- Cards: White background, green hover border
- Section tags: Uppercase, letter-spaced, green with line
- Headings: Bebas Neue, dark green
- Body text: DM Sans, gray

## Do NOT include
- Customer testimonials (not yet available)
- Pricing information
- Team member names (only roles)
- Oakland or East Bay references
- North Bay references (only SF and Marin County)
