The California HVAC license is the state's specific credential for Warm-Air Heating, Ventilating and Air-Conditioning contractors. Solid Bay Construction SF holds this license — meaning every HVAC job we perform is legally compliant, properly permitted, and covered by your homeowner's insurance.

Many SF and Marin County homeowners unknowingly hire unlicensed HVAC contractors — only to discover that the work isn't covered by insurance when something goes wrong. Always verify a HVAC license at cslb.ca.gov before hiring any HVAC contractor.
