COMMON HVAC QUESTIONS

How much does heat pump installation cost in Marin County?
Heat pump installation in Marin County typically costs $10,000–$20,000 before rebates. After TECH Clean California, federal IRA, and Marin Clean Energy incentives, net cost is often $5,000–$12,000.
What HVAC rebates are available in Marin County?
Marin County homeowners can access TECH Clean California (up to $4,500), federal IRA tax credits (up to $2,000), PG&E rebates, and additional Marin Clean Energy incentives. Total can exceed $10,000.
Which Marin County cities does SolidBay serve for HVAC?
We serve all Marin County cities — Tiburon, Belvedere, Mill Valley, Sausalito, San Rafael, Corte Madera, Larkspur, Kentfield, Ross, Marin City, and Strawberry.
Do I need permits for HVAC installation in Marin County?
Yes — all Marin County cities require permits for HVAC installation. Our expeditor is familiar with each city's building department and handles all submissions.
