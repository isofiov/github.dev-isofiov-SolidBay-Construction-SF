Marin County ADU FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS
FROM MARIN HOMEOWNERS

Can I build an ADU on my Marin County property?

Yes. Under current California law, most single-family properties in Marin County can build one ADU and one Junior ADU by right. Some hillside or septic-dependent lots require additional engineering review, which our team handles in-house.

How much does a Marin County ADU cost?

Garage conversions typically run $180,000–$350,000. Detached new-construction ADUs run $350,000–$600,000+ depending on size and site conditions.

How long does an ADU take to build in Marin?

Design and permitting typically take 4–8 months; construction takes 4–8 months depending on ADU type. Total timeline is usually 9–16 months start to finish.

Do you handle septic or well system requirements for Marin ADUs?

Yes — our in-house structural engineers coordinate with septic and well specialists for Marin properties outside sewer service areas, which is common in parts of Mill Valley and unincorporated Marin.
