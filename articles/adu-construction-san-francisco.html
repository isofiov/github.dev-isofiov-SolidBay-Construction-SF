An Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) is one of the most powerful investments a San Francisco homeowner can make. Thanks to California's 2020–2022 ADU law reforms, building an ADU in SF is faster, cheaper, and easier than ever before — and the financial returns are extraordinary in SF's rental market.

What is an ADU?

An ADU is a self-contained residential unit on the same lot as a single-family or multi-family home. It includes its own kitchen, bathroom, and living space. Common types in SF include garage conversions, basement conversions, detached backyard cottages, and additions above garages.

ADU Rules in San Francisco (2026)

Under California state law (SB 9, AB 2221, and AB 976), most SF single-family properties can now build:

  • One ADU (attached, detached, or garage conversion)
  • One Junior ADU (JADU — up to 500 sq ft within the primary home)
  • No minimum lot size requirement for ADUs
  • No owner-occupancy requirement (as of 2025)
  • Ministerial approval — no discretionary hearing required

Key Change: SF can no longer deny ADU applications that meet objective standards. The city must approve qualifying ADUs ministerially — meaning no neighborhood opposition, no Planning Commission hearings, and no discretionary review.

Why Garage Conversions Are the Most Popular ADU in SF

Garage conversions are the most cost-effective ADU in San Francisco because:

  • The structure already exists — no new foundation or framing from scratch
  • Utilities are often nearby (electrical panel, water lines)
  • Typically below grade — natural temperature regulation
  • Fastest permit approval timeline
  • Most SF garages are underutilized (cars are rarely parked in SF garages)

ADU Financial Returns in San Francisco

SF's rental market makes ADUs exceptionally lucrative:

  • A 1-bedroom garage conversion ADU in SF rents for $3,000–$4,500/month
  • A 2-bedroom detached ADU can rent for $4,500–$6,500/month
  • ADUs add $250,000–$500,000+ to SF home values
  • Typical payback period: 6–12 years through rental income

Best ADU Designs for San Francisco Properties

The Garage Studio

Convert a 1- or 2-car garage into a studio or 1-bedroom ADU with a full kitchen and bathroom. Most popular in the Mission, Noe Valley, and Bernal Heights. Cost: $180K–$250K.

The Garden Cottage

Build a standalone cottage in the backyard. Works best on larger Marin County lots or SF properties with rear yard space. Offers the most privacy for both occupants. Cost: $300K–$500K.

The Basement Apartment

Many SF Victorian and Edwardian homes have partially finished basements perfect for ADU conversion. Requires careful attention to egress, waterproofing, and ceiling height. Cost: $200K–$320K.
