WHY MINI-SPLITS ARE PERFECT FOR SAN FRANCISCO HOMES

San Francisco's housing stock presents unique HVAC challenges that make mini-splits particularly attractive. Most SF homes were built before central heating was standard — Victorians and Edwardians typically have radiant heat (steam or hot water radiators) with no ductwork at all. Installing a traditional central AC system in these homes would require extensive — and expensive — new ductwork installation. A mini-split system requires only a small 3-inch hole through the wall to connect the indoor and outdoor units, avoiding the cost and disruption of ductwork installation entirely. Mini-splits are also ideal for San Francisco's ADU boom. A garage conversion or new detached ADU needs its own independent HVAC system — and a single-zone mini-split provides heating and cooling for typically $3,500–$6,000 installed, significantly less than extending a central system. For room additions, mini-splits provide precise temperature control for the new space without stressing your existing HVAC system. And because mini-splits are heat pumps, they qualify for the same federal and state rebates — making them an even more attractive investment.

MINI-SPLIT COSTS AND BRANDS IN SAN FRANCISCO

Mini-split installation costs in San Francisco range from $3,500–$8,000 for a single-zone system and $8,000–$20,000 for multi-zone systems covering 2–5 rooms. Key cost factors include the number of zones (each additional indoor unit adds $1,500–$3,000), the capacity required (BTU sizing based on room size), electrical panel capacity (some installations require panel upgrades), and wall penetration complexity (exterior walls vs interior walls, first floor vs upper floors). We install all major mini-split brands: Mitsubishi Electric (our top recommendation — the most reliable and efficient brand available), Daikin, Fujitsu, LG, and Samsung. Mitsubishi Hyper-Heat models are particularly popular in SF for their excellent performance in cold, foggy conditions and their quiet operation. All mini-split installations include permits, proper electrical work, and manufacturer registration for warranty purposes.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How many mini-split zones do I need for my San Francisco home?
Most SF homes need 1 zone per floor or per area with different usage patterns. A 3-bedroom Victorian might use 3 zones — living area, primary bedroom, and guest room/office. We'll assess your specific home and recommend the right configuration.
What is the best mini-split brand for San Francisco?
We recommend Mitsubishi Electric for most SF applications — particularly their Hyper-Heat models. They perform exceptionally well in SF's cool, damp climate and have the best reliability record in the industry.
Do mini-splits qualify for rebates in San Francisco?
Yes — ductless mini-split heat pumps qualify for the federal IRA tax credit (up to $2,000) and TECH Clean California rebates (up to $3,000). Combined savings of $4,000–$5,000 are achievable on a qualifying installation.
How long does mini-split installation take in San Francisco?
A single-zone mini-split installation typically takes 4–8 hours. Multi-zone systems take 1–2 days. Permits typically add 1–2 weeks before installation can begin.
