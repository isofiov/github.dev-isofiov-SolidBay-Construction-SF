WHY BUILD AN ADU IN MARIN COUNTY?

Marin County homeowners are increasingly choosing to build ADUs as property values rise and rental demand grows. A well-built ADU in Mill Valley, Tiburon, or San Rafael can generate $2,500–$5,000/month in rental income while adding 15–25% to your property value. Under current California law (AB 2221 and SB 897), most single-family properties in Marin County can build one ADU and one Junior ADU by right — meaning the city cannot deny a compliant application. Solid Bay specializes in navigating Marin County's local planning requirements, ensuring your ADU project gets approved and built efficiently.

Marin County ADU projects typically cost $180,000–$450,000 depending on type and finish level. Garage conversions are the most cost-effective option ($120,000–$220,000), while detached new-construction ADUs run $250,000–$450,000. Our in-house architects design your ADU to maximize livable space within your lot's constraints, and our permit expeditor handles all Marin County building department submissions. We serve Tiburon, Mill Valley, Sausalito, San Rafael, Corte Madera, Larkspur, Kentfield, Ross, Marin City, and Strawberry.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How much does an ADU cost in Marin County?
ADU costs in Marin County typically range from $120,000–$220,000 for garage conversions and $250,000–$450,000 for new detached units, depending on size and finish level.
Do I need a permit for an ADU in Marin County?
Yes — all ADU construction requires building permits. However, under current California law, most ADU applications must be approved if they meet objective zoning standards. Our expeditor handles all permit applications.
How long does it take to build an ADU in Marin County?
From design to move-in, a Marin County ADU typically takes 9–14 months — approximately 2–3 months for design and permits, and 6–9 months for construction.
Can I rent my Marin County ADU on Airbnb?
Short-term rental rules vary by city. Long-term rentals (12+ month leases) are allowed in most Marin County cities. We recommend confirming local short-term rental ordinances with your city before planning for Airbnb use.
