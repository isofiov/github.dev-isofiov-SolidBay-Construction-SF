WHAT TO LOOK FOR IN A MARIN COUNTY HVAC CONTRACTOR

Marin County homeowners should verify several things before hiring any HVAC contractor. First and most important: HVAC license verification. The California HVAC license is specifically for Warm-Air Heating, Ventilating and Air-Conditioning contractors. Verify any HVAC contractor's license at the CSLB website (cslb.ca.gov) before signing a contract. Unlicensed HVAC work voids your homeowner's insurance and can create liability if there is a fire or carbon monoxide incident. Second: insurance verification. Your contractor must carry general liability insurance and workers' compensation — ask for certificates before work begins. Third: permit history. A reputable Marin County HVAC contractor pulls permits on every installation. You can verify permit history on your county's building department portal. Fourth: manufacturer certifications. Factory-authorized dealers for brands like Carrier, Trane, and Lennox receive better warranty support and training. Solid Bay meets all four criteria — licensed, fully insured, permit on every job, and certified by multiple major HVAC brands.

How do I verify an HVAC contractor's license in Marin County?
Visit cslb.ca.gov and search the contractor's license number or business name. Verify the license is active, the classification includes Lic. #717881, and the bond and insurance are current.
What HVAC permits are required in Marin County cities?
Each Marin city has its own building department. All require mechanical permits for HVAC installation. Some cities (like Tiburon and San Rafael) have additional energy code requirements. Solid Bay handles permits in all Marin cities.
