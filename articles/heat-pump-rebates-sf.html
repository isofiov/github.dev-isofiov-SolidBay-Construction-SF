COMPLETE HEAT PUMP REBATE GUIDE FOR SAN FRANCISCO

Here is a complete breakdown of all available heat pump rebates for San Francisco homeowners in 2026. Federal Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit: 30% of the cost of a heat pump, up to $2,000 per year. This is a tax credit — it directly reduces your federal tax bill. Available to all income levels. No application required — just file IRS Form 5695 with your tax return. TECH Clean California Rebates: $1,000–$4,500 for ducted heat pump systems replacing gas heating. $500–$3,000 for ductless mini-split heat pumps. Available to all California residents regardless of income. Applied at point of sale — Solid Bay processes the rebate paperwork for you. PG&E Energy Efficiency Rebates: Up to $1,000 for qualifying heat pump HVAC systems. Available to PG&E residential customers. Requires installation by a licensed contractor (we qualify). Bayside Climate Initiative (SF-specific): Additional incentives for SF residents switching from gas to all-electric. Check sf.gov for current program details. Combined total potential savings: $7,500–$10,500+ depending on system type and your tax situation.

HOW TO MAXIMIZE YOUR HEAT PUMP REBATES IN SAN FRANCISCO

Getting the maximum rebates requires some planning. First, make sure you choose qualifying equipment — systems must meet efficiency minimums (typically HSPF2 7.5 or higher for heat pumps). Second, keep all receipts and documentation — the IRA credit requires proof of purchase and installation costs. Third, apply for utility rebates promptly — PG&E and TECH Clean California rebates sometimes have annual caps that can run out. Fourth, time your installation for the tax year when you'll benefit most from the $2,000 federal credit. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can I get heat pump rebates if I rent my home in San Francisco?
Most heat pump rebates are available to homeowners only. Renters typically cannot access the IRA tax credit or TECH Clean California rebates, as these require ownership of the property where the system is installed.
Do heat pump rebates stack in San Francisco?
Yes — federal, state, and utility rebates can be stacked. You can claim the IRA tax credit, TECH Clean California rebate, and PG&E rebate on the same installation, potentially totaling $7,500 or more.
How long does it take to receive heat pump rebates in SF?
The federal IRA credit is claimed on your annual tax return — you receive it as a tax reduction when you file. TECH Clean California rebates are typically applied within 6–8 weeks of installation. PG&E rebates take 4–8 weeks.
