Home Addition Guide for San Francisco Homeowners

By Solid Bay Construction SF | Updated 2026

With SF median home prices above $1.3M, expanding the home you have is often more cost-effective than moving. Here's everything you need to know about adding square footage in San Francisco.

Types of Home Additions in SF

  • Horizontal rear additions — most common in SF, extending into backyard
  • Vertical additions — adding a floor to existing structure
  • Pop-top additions — raising the roofline to add headroom
  • Bump-outs — small extensions of 50–200 sq ft

Home Addition Costs in San Francisco

  • Horizontal rear addition: $400 – $700 per sq ft
  • 500 sq ft addition: $200,000 – $350,000
  • Vertical addition (new floor): $500 – $800 per sq ft
  • Bump-out (100 sq ft): $60,000 – $120,000

Permits for Home Additions in SF

All additions require permits from both DBI and typically SF Planning. Rear additions under certain size thresholds may qualify for over-the-counter approval. Our expeditor manages the entire process.

Home Addition Timeline

  • Design: 2–4 months
  • Permitting: 3–6 months
  • Construction: 4–8 months
  • Total: 9–18 months start to finish

Is a Home Addition Worth It in SF?

Adding 500 sq ft to an SF home typically adds $500,000 – $800,000 in market value — often 2–3x the cost of construction. It's one of the highest-ROI investments available to SF homeowners.
