Building an ADU for Aging Parents or Multigenerational Living

By Solid Bay Construction SF | Updated 2026

Most ADU content focuses on rental income potential. But a large share of the ADUs we build in San Francisco and Marin aren't rentals at all — they're for an aging parent, an adult child, or a caregiver living close to family. That changes the design priorities significantly.

How a Family ADU Differs From a Rental ADU

  • Accessibility from day one: Zero-threshold entry, a curbless shower, and wider doorways aren't optional add-ons — they're often the entire point, especially for an aging parent
  • Proximity and connection: A family ADU often benefits from a covered walkway or visual sightline to the main house, rather than being tucked at the back of the lot for maximum privacy like a rental unit would be
  • Kitchen scope: Some family ADUs skip the full kitchen required for a legal rental and use a kitchenette instead — but check with your jurisdiction, since California's ADU law generally still requires basic cooking facilities for the unit to qualify as an ADU under state law
  • Emergency response access: Consider whether paramedics could access the unit with a gurney if needed — door widths and turning radius matter more here than in a typical rental

California ADU Law Basics

Under California Government Code §65852.2, cities generally cannot require owner-occupancy of the primary residence for most ADUs (a change from older rules), cannot impose minimum lot size requirements for a single ADU, and must allow at least one ADU by right on most residential lots — which is why family ADUs have become such a common solution for multigenerational households in expensive markets like SF and Marin.

Financing Considerations

Because a family ADU isn't generating rental income, some homeowners use a HELOC or cash-out refinance rather than lender programs that specifically underwrite against projected rental income. Factor this into your budget conversation early, since it can affect the scope you can afford.
