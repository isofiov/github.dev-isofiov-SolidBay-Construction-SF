What Does Demolition Cost in a San Francisco Renovation?

By Solid Bay Construction SF | Updated 2026

Demolition is the first phase homeowners actually see — and in San Francisco, it's rarely the bargain people expect. Between disposal fees, potential asbestos survey requirements, and the city's density, demo costs more here than almost anywhere else in the country.

Typical Demolition Costs by Scope

ScopeTypical Cost
Single room (kitchen or bath) strip-out$3,000–$8,000
Interior wall removal (non-structural)$1,500–$4,000
Full interior gut, single unit$15,000–$35,000
Full structure demolition (rebuild)$40,000–$100,000+

What Drives SF Demo Costs Higher

  • Disposal and dump fees — landfill tipping fees in the Bay Area run well above the national average, and every ton removed from a job site is billed
  • Asbestos and lead surveys — required before demo permits are issued on most pre-1980 buildings, adding both cost and schedule time before crews can start
  • Limited access — narrow lots, shared driveways, and street parking permits for debris bins slow the physical removal process
  • Hand demolition — in dense, older buildings, much of the work has to be done by hand rather than machine to protect adjacent structures and utilities, which costs more in labor

What's Usually Included

A proper demolition bid should include debris bin rental, hauling and disposal fees, any required hazmat survey and abatement coordination, and protection of areas not being demolished (flooring, adjacent rooms). If a bid seems unusually low, confirm disposal fees aren't being quoted separately later.

Demolition Isn't Where to Cut Corners

It's tempting to save money with an unlicensed demo crew, but demolition is also when hidden issues — old wiring, water damage, structural surprises — get discovered. Having your general contractor's own crew or a licensed subcontractor handle demo means those discoveries get documented and addressed immediately, rather than creating disputes later about what was already there.
