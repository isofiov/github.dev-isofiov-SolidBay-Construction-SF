The BRRRR Strategy for San Francisco Real Estate Investors

By Solid Bay Construction SF | Updated 2026

Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat — BRRRR is a well-known investment strategy nationally, but San Francisco's permitting rules, rent control, and construction costs change the math significantly compared to lower-cost markets. Here's how it actually plays out for multi-unit and ADU-eligible properties in the city.

The Five Steps, SF-Adjusted

  • Buy: Target a property with rehab or ADU upside that the current condition or configuration is masking — a dated multi-unit building, a single-family home with legal ADU potential, or a property with deferred maintenance scaring off owner-occupant buyers
  • Rehab: Renovate units and/or add legal square footage (an ADU, a legalized in-law unit, a second story) — permitted, not cosmetic-only, since permitted square footage is what an appraiser and a future lender will recognize
  • Rent: Stabilize the property at market rent — being mindful that SF Rent Ordinance protections apply to most buildings with units occupied before June 1979, which affects turnover timing and how renovations are sequenced around existing tenancies
  • Refinance: Once rented and stabilized, refinance based on the new appraised value (and for multi-unit properties, often the new income approach valuation) to recover rehab capital
  • Repeat: Redeploy the recovered capital into the next acquisition

Where This Strategy Works Best

BRRRR tends to work best in San Francisco on multi-unit buildings with genuine rehab or ADU upside — not single-unit cosmetic flips, where the margin between rehab cost and appraised value increase is usually too thin. Properties where a legal ADU or JADU can be added, or where a dated multi-unit building can be brought up to current rental standards, are where the "rehab creates value beyond its cost" math has the best chance of working in this market.

Not financial, tax, or lending advice. Rent control applicability, income-approach appraisal outcomes, and refinance qualification all vary by property and lender. Work with a licensed lender, and where relevant a real estate attorney or tax professional, before committing to this strategy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does BRRRR work in San Francisco given how expensive property is?

It can, though the math is tighter than in lower-cost markets. It works best on multi-unit properties and properties with legal ADU potential, not single cosmetic flips.

What San Francisco-specific rules affect a BRRRR renovation on a multi-unit building?

Rent control applies to most buildings with units occupied before June 1979, and soft-story retrofit requirements can apply to older wood-frame multi-unit buildings — both should be factored into your rehab budget and timeline.
