Novato Home Remodeling & Addition Guide

By Solid Bay Construction SF | Updated 2026

Novato is Marin County's northernmost city, and its housing stock and lot sizes are noticeably different from San Francisco or southern Marin — which changes what a typical renovation or addition looks like here.

What Makes Novato Different

Compared to SF's narrow lots and southern Marin's hillside parcels, Novato has a larger share of single-story ranch homes from the 1960s–70s on flatter, more generous lots. That opens up addition types — like a simple rear or side bump-out — that are harder to pull off on a typical SF row-house lot, but it also means many homes need a full systems update (electrical, plumbing, insulation) as part of any significant remodel.

Novato Permitting

Homes within Novato's city limits go through the City of Novato's own Community Development Department — a separate building division from both San Francisco's DBI and unincorporated Marin County. If you're near the city boundary, confirming jurisdiction before you design is worth the extra step, since setback rules, ADU allowances, and review timelines differ between the city and the county.

Novato Neighborhoods at a Glance

  • Ignacio: Mix of mid-century ranch homes and newer construction near Hamilton
  • Hamilton: Former air base redeveloped into planned residential — newer homes, more consistent HOA design review
  • Downtown Novato / Old Town: Older, smaller-lot homes closer to the historic core
  • Black Point-Green Point: Waterfront and near-waterfront properties with their own flood and setback considerations
  • Novato Knolls / Pleasant Valley: Larger hillside lots with more room for additions and ADUs
