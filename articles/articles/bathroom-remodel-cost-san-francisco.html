What Drives Bathroom Remodel Costs in San Francisco?

Tile Work

Tile is one of the biggest variables in bathroom costs. Basic subway tile runs $15–$25/sq ft installed. Custom or large-format tile can reach $60–$100/sq ft installed. A 60 sq ft bathroom with tile on all surfaces can have $8,000–$30,000+ in tile costs alone.

Plumbing

SF plumbers charge $150–$200/hour. Relocating a toilet, shower, or tub adds significantly to your budget — typically $3,000–$8,000 per fixture moved. SF's aging pipe infrastructure means you'll often need to replace galvanized supply lines with copper or PEX, adding $2,000–$5,000.

Waterproofing

Proper waterproofing is non-negotiable in SF's climate. Modern systems like Schluter or Wedi add $2,000–$5,000 but prevent costly water damage repairs down the line. Never skip this.

Permits

Any plumbing change, electrical update, or structural modification requires permits from SF DBI. Budget $1,500–$4,000 for bathroom permit fees.

How to Save Money on a San Francisco Bathroom Remodel

Keep Plumbing in Place

The single biggest cost-saver is not moving plumbing. If you can keep your toilet, shower, and vanity in their current locations, you'll save $5,000–$15,000.

Choose Mid-Range Tile

Beautiful, durable tile is available at $8–$15/sq ft. You don't need $50/sq ft imported marble for an excellent result. Save the high-end tile for a feature wall or shower floor.

Standard Fixture Sizing

Custom or oversized fixtures require custom framing and additional labor. Standard 60" tub/shower units and standard-depth vanities significantly reduce installation complexity.

Do You Need Permits for a Bathroom Remodel in SF?

Permits are required for:

  • Moving any plumbing fixture
  • Adding or upgrading electrical circuits (GFCI, exhaust fan)
  • Structural changes (removing walls)
  • Adding a bathroom where none existed

Cosmetic updates — like retiling, replacing fixtures in the same locations, or new vanity — generally don't require permits in SF. But always confirm with your contractor before assuming.

Why Bathroom Remodels Go Over Budget in SF

  • Hidden water damage discovered during demolition
  • Mold remediation requirements
  • Outdated electrical requiring full panel upgrade
  • Asbestos in older tile or flooring
  • Structural issues in walls or floor

Always budget a 15% contingency for bathroom remodels in older SF homes. In our experience, about 60% of SF bathroom projects encounter at least one hidden condition that adds cost.
