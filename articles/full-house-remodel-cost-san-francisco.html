How Much Does a Full House Remodel Cost in San Francisco?

By Solid Bay Construction SF | Updated 2026

A full house remodel in San Francisco typically runs $700,000–$1.5M for a 2,000–2,800 sq ft home — a wide range, because "full remodel" covers everything from a cosmetic refresh of an already-sound house to a complete gut with structural, seismic, and system work. Here's how to know where your project actually falls.

Cost Per Square Foot by Finish Level

ScopeCost per sq ft
Light cosmetic (paint, flooring, fixtures)$150–$250
Mid-level renovation (some layout changes)$300–$500
High-end renovation (open floor plan, new systems)$500–$800
Luxury / custom (full gut, premium finishes)$800–$1,200+

What Actually Drives the Number Up

Structural changes

Removing load-bearing walls, adding steel beams, or reconfiguring the floor plan adds engineering, permitting, and construction cost beyond simple finish work. Load-bearing wall removal alone typically runs $25,000–$60,000 including a new beam and engineering.

Seismic and foundation work

Many SF homes built before 1950 need cripple wall bracing, foundation bolting, or a fuller foundation retrofit as part of a full remodel — typically $8,000–$25,000 for voluntary seismic upgrades, or $15,000–$80,000+ if foundation replacement is involved.

Full system replacement

Rewiring an entire home runs $40,000–$100,000+; a full re-pipe and HVAC replacement add further cost. Older SF housing stock — often 80–100+ years old — frequently needs all three (electrical, plumbing, HVAC) replaced together during a full remodel.

Permitting complexity

A full remodel touching structural, electrical, and plumbing systems requires DBI plan check (4–10 weeks) and sometimes Planning Department review (8–16 weeks) — permit expediting isn't a line item that shows up in "cost per square foot," but it affects how long your money is committed before construction even starts.

A Realistic Budget Range

For a typical 2,000–2,800 sq ft San Francisco home undergoing a genuinely full remodel — new kitchen, all bathrooms, updated systems, some structural work — expect $700,000–$1.5M all-in. Larger homes, Victorian-specific challenges, or added square footage push toward the top of that range and beyond.
