Earthquake Retrofit in San Francisco: What Homeowners Need to Know

By Solid Bay Construction SF | Updated 2026

San Francisco sits at the intersection of multiple major fault systems. Understanding your retrofit obligations — and opportunities — is essential for every SF homeowner.

SF's Mandatory Soft-Story Retrofit Program

San Francisco's Mandatory Soft-Story Retrofit Ordinance requires certain wood-frame residential buildings — typically 3+ stories with soft ground floors — to be seismically retrofitted. Check your compliance deadline at sfdbi.org.

Voluntary Seismic Upgrades

  • Cripple wall bracing: $8,000 – $20,000
  • Foundation anchor bolts: $3,000 – $8,000
  • Full soft-story retrofit: $50,000 – $150,000

Combining Retrofit With Renovation

The most cost-effective time to do seismic work is during a renovation — when walls are already open. Solid Bay integrates seismic upgrades into renovation scope, saving mobilization costs and minimizing disruption.

The Retrofit Process

  • Structural assessment by licensed engineer
  • Design of retrofit solution
  • Permit submittal to DBI
  • Construction — typically 2–6 weeks
  • Final inspection and sign-off

Does Retrofit Add Value?

Yes — a seismically retrofitted home is more insurable, more marketable, and more attractive to buyers. Many SF buyers specifically ask about retrofit status.
