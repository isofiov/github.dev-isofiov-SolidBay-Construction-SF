Bathroom Remodel Cost in San Francisco: 2026 Pricing Guide

By SolidBay Construction SF | Updated 2026

Bathroom remodels are one of the most popular home improvement projects in San Francisco — and one of the best ways to add value to your property.

Average Bathroom Remodel Costs in SF

  • Small bathroom refresh: $15,000 – $30,000
  • Mid-range full remodel: $30,000 – $70,000
  • Luxury master bath: $70,000 – $150,000+

What Drives Up Costs in SF?

  • Premium labor rates for plumbers, tile setters, electricians
  • SF DBI permit fees: $2,000 – $8,000+
  • Old galvanized or cast iron pipes needing replacement
  • Tight Victorian-era floor plans requiring custom work

Do You Need a Permit?

Yes for moving plumbing fixtures, adding electrical outlets, or any structural wall changes. Cosmetic updates like replacing a vanity typically don't require permits.

How Long Does It Take?

  • Small refresh: 1–2 weeks
  • Full remodel: 3–6 weeks + permit wait
  • Luxury remodel: 6–12 weeks

Tips to Stay on Budget

  • Keep plumbing fixtures in existing locations
  • Choose in-stock tile over custom imports
  • Get 3 detailed written quotes before deciding
  • Set aside 15–20% contingency budget
