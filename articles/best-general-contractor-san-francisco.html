How to Find the Best General Contractor in San Francisco

By SolidBay Construction SF | Updated 2026

San Francisco has thousands of contractors — ranging from exceptional to outright fraudulent. Here's how to find the right one for your project.

What to Look For

  • Valid California CSLB license — verify at cslb.ca.gov
  • General liability insurance ($1M+ per occurrence)
  • Workers' compensation insurance
  • Experience with SF permits and DBI inspections
  • SF project references you can actually call
  • Detailed written contracts with itemized scope

Red Flags to Avoid

  • Asking for more than 10% or $1,000 as a deposit (illegal in California)
  • No physical address or verifiable license
  • Pushing to skip permits
  • Vague contracts without itemized scope
  • No insurance certificates
  • Pressure to decide immediately

Why In-House Teams Matter in SF

SF's complex permitting environment means your contractor needs more than construction skills. A firm with in-house architects, structural engineers, and a permit expeditor saves significant time and money.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring

  • How many SF projects have you completed in the last 2 years?
  • Who pulls the permits — you or a subcontractor?
  • Do you have an in-house expeditor?
  • Can I speak with 3 recent SF clients?
  • What's your process when unexpected conditions arise?
