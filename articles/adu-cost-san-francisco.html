ADU Cost in San Francisco: Complete 2026 Pricing Breakdown

By Solid Bay Construction SF | Updated 2026

ADU cost in San Francisco varies more by type than almost any other renovation project — a garage conversion, a basement unit, and new detached construction start from very different baselines. Here's what each actually costs.

ADU Cost by Type

ADU TypeTypical Cost
Garage conversion ADU$180,000–$350,000
Basement conversion ADU$200,000–$400,000
New detached (backyard) ADU$350,000–$550,000+
Junior ADU (JADU, up to 500 sq ft, within existing structure)$80,000–$180,000

Why Garage Conversions Are the Most Cost-Effective ADU

A garage conversion starts with an existing foundation, roof, and structure already in place — you're building out interior systems and finishes rather than starting from bare dirt. That's why it remains the lowest-cost path to a legal ADU for most SF homeowners, and why it's consistently our most-requested ADU project type.

What Drives ADU Cost Within Each Type

  • Foundation condition — an older garage often needs foundation work before conversion, which adds $15,000–$80,000+ depending on scope
  • Utility distance — how far the ADU sits from existing sewer, water, and electrical service points affects trenching and connection cost
  • Kitchen vs. kitchenette — a full kitchen (required for a standard ADU, not a JADU) adds plumbing, electrical, and ventilation cost over a JADU's kitchenette
  • Finish level — rental-grade finishes vs. a unit built to match the main house's finish level can swing the budget by $50,000–$100,000+

ADU Return on Investment

A legal ADU typically adds $200,000–$500,000+ in property value in San Francisco — on top of whatever rental income it generates while you own the home. Combined with SF's by-right ADU approval process (no discretionary hearing required for most properties), it's consistently one of the highest-ROI projects we build.
