TYPES OF HOME ADDITIONS IN MARIN COUNTY

The most common home addition types we build in Marin County include rear additions (extending the back of your home into the yard — the most popular and cost-effective option), second-story additions (adding a full or partial upper level — ideal when lot coverage is maxed out), bump-out additions (smaller expansions of 100–400 sq ft for a specific room like a kitchen or bathroom), and primary suite additions (master bedroom, bathroom, and walk-in closet additions — extremely popular in Marin). Each type requires careful consideration of your lot coverage allowances, setback requirements, and the specific regulations of your Marin County city.

Home addition costs in Marin County typically run $400–$700/sq ft for mid-range work and $700–$1,200/sq ft for luxury finishes. A 400 sq ft rear addition might cost $180,000–$300,000. A full second-story addition of 800 sq ft might cost $500,000–$900,000. Our in-house structural engineers design addition foundations and framing, our architects handle permit drawings, and our project managers coordinate every subcontractor. We serve all Marin County cities — Tiburon, Mill Valley, Sausalito, San Rafael, Corte Madera, Larkspur, Kentfield, Ross, and more.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How much does a home addition cost in Marin County?
Home additions in Marin County typically cost $400–$700/sq ft for mid-range and $700–$1,200/sq ft for luxury. A 400 sq ft addition runs $180,000–$300,000.
How long does a home addition take in Marin County?
A typical Marin County home addition takes 9–15 months including design (2–3 months), permitting (2–4 months), and construction (5–8 months).
Do I need planning approval for a home addition in Marin County?
Yes — additions require both building permits and often planning department review depending on size and location. Our team manages the entire approval process.
Can I add a second story to my Marin County home?
In most cases yes — but second-story additions are subject to height limits and neighbor noticing requirements that vary by city. Our architects assess feasibility before design begins.
