Kitchen Remodeling in San Francisco: Everything Homeowners Need to Know

By SolidBay Construction SF | Updated 2026

A kitchen remodel is one of the highest-ROI investments a San Francisco homeowner can make. Whether you're updating a cramped galley kitchen in the Mission or doing a full gut renovation in Pacific Heights, understanding the process — costs, permits, timelines, and design — is the key to a successful project.

How Much Does a Kitchen Remodel Cost in San Francisco?

  • Basic remodel: $40,000 – $70,000
  • Mid-range remodel: $70,000 – $130,000
  • High-end remodel: $130,000 – $250,000+

Do You Need a Permit?

Yes — the SF Department of Building Inspection (DBI) requires permits for plumbing, electrical, structural, and gas line work.

How Long Does It Take?

  • Design & planning: 2–6 weeks
  • Permit approval: 4–12 weeks
  • Construction: 4–10 weeks

2026 Kitchen Trends in SF

  • Induction cooktops replacing gas
  • Quartz and sintered stone countertops
  • Warm wood tones with matte black hardware
  • Integrated smart home appliances
