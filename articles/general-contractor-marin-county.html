WHAT TO LOOK FOR IN A MARIN COUNTY GENERAL CONTRACTOR

The best Marin County general contractors share several key characteristics. First, proper licensing — California requires all general contractors to hold an active CSLB license (Class B). Second, comprehensive insurance — general liability and workers' compensation insurance protect you if anything goes wrong on your property. Third, local experience — Marin County's building departments, planning commissions, and geological conditions are unique. A contractor who has completed multiple projects in your specific city will navigate the process far more efficiently than one new to the area. Fourth, in-house expertise — contractors who employ architects, engineers, and designers in-house can integrate design and construction seamlessly, reducing costs and improving quality. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is Solid Bay licensed to work in all Marin County cities?
Yes — our California CSLB Class B General Building license covers all of Marin County. We regularly work in Tiburon, Mill Valley, Sausalito, San Rafael, Corte Madera, Larkspur, Kentfield, and Ross.
How do I verify a contractor's license in Marin County?
Visit the California CSLB website (cslb.ca.gov) and search the contractor's license number. Always verify that the license is active and matches the business name before signing a contract.
What permits does a Marin County contractor typically pull?
Building permits, plumbing permits, electrical permits, and mechanical permits are the most common. Each Marin city has its own building department. Our expeditor is familiar with all of them.
