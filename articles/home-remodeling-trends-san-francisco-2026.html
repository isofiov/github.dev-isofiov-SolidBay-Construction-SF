TOP 7 HOME REMODELING TRENDS IN SAN FRANCISCO FOR 2026

Discover the top 7 home remodeling trends in San Francisco for 2026 — from kitchen-living integration to ADU construction and primary suite upgrades.

Current Trends

San Francisco homeowners are investing in their properties at record rates in 2026. Driven by high equity values, remote work, and a desire for homes that truly work for modern life, SF renovation projects are getting bigger, smarter, and more design-forward than ever.

1. Kitchen-Living Integration

The closed Victorian kitchen is disappearing fast. SF homeowners are removing walls between kitchens, dining rooms, and living areas at a rapid pace — creating open-plan spaces that feel larger and work better for entertaining. This typically involves structural engineering (many of these walls are load-bearing), permits, and a budget of $40,000–$80,000 for the structural work alone. The transformation is dramatic.

Design Tip: When opening an SF Victorian kitchen, preserving original architectural details — bay windows, ceiling moldings, original floors — in the newly combined space maintains the character that makes SF homes special and valuable.

2. ADU Construction Surge

California's ADU law reforms have created an ADU construction boom in SF. Garage conversions, backyard cottages, and basement apartments are being built at unprecedented rates. The financial logic is compelling: a $220,000 garage conversion generates $3,500–$4,500/month in rent — a 20%+ annual return on the investment.

3. Permanent Home Office Spaces

Post-pandemic, SF homeowners are converting dedicated spaces into proper home offices — not temporary setups. We're seeing custom built-ins, acoustic treatments, dedicated HVAC zones, and professional-grade lighting in projects averaging $35,000–$75,000.

4. Primary Suite Upgrades

The primary bathroom is getting luxury treatment across SF. Steam showers, heated floors, freestanding soaking tubs, and custom tile work are standard in high-end primary bath projects. Homeowners are treating their primary suite as a personal retreat — with budgets of $90,000–$180,000 becoming common for top-tier projects.

5. Outdoor Living Expansion

SF's mild climate makes outdoor living possible year-round with the right setup. Decks, patios, outdoor kitchens, and covered pergolas are growing in popularity — especially in Marin County and SF's hillside neighborhoods with views. Projects range from $40,000 for a simple deck to $150,000+ for a full outdoor kitchen with pergola and heating.

6. Seismic Retrofitting as Part of Renovation Packages

SF homeowners are increasingly bundling seismic retrofitting with larger renovation projects — both for safety and for insurance savings. Combining a seismic retrofit with a kitchen or bathroom remodel allows shared permit costs and contractor mobilization. Retrofit cost when bundled: $10,000–$25,000.

7. Sustainability and Full Electrification

All-electric upgrades, solar installations, and heat pump systems are increasingly popular in SF renovations — driven by building code changes, PG&E rate increases, and environmental consciousness. Many homeowners combine kitchen replacements or HVAC upgrades with full electrification packages.
