Home Remodeling Trends in San Francisco for 2026

By SolidBay Construction SF | Updated 2026

San Francisco homeowners and contractors are embracing a new wave of design, material, and technology trends in 2026. Here's what we're seeing across our projects.

Top Design Trends

  • Warm earthy color palettes replacing all-white interiors
  • Zellige and handmade tile for bathrooms and kitchens
  • Fluted wood and stone details
  • Indoor-outdoor living with folding glass walls

Material Trends

  • White oak and walnut hardwood flooring
  • Sintered stone and quartzite countertops
  • Venetian plaster walls
  • Unlacquered brass and matte black fixtures

Technology Trends

  • All-electric homes — heat pump HVAC replacing gas
  • EV charging circuits as standard
  • Smart home integration (Lutron, Crestron)
  • Solar-ready electrical panels

SF-Specific Trends

  • ADU additions for rental income or multigenerational living
  • Seismic upgrades rolled into renovation scope
  • Victorian restoration with modern systems
  • Small space optimization for compact SF floor plans
